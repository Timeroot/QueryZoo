\(\overline f_{\mathrm{AIK}}\)

Complement of the Ambainis–Iraids–Kokainis DNF

Logarithm-free quadratic $\mathrm{C}$ versus certain-acceptance one-sided $\mathrm{R}^1$ separation.

total composed arity: $N=n^2$ bits

Definition not yet transcribed. This page gives a pointer and an informal description only; the exact mathematical definition still needs to be written into data/functions/07-recent-separations.yaml under formal:. Follow the references below.

Description

Take the Boolean complement of \(\mathrm{AIKuD}\). The AIK one-sided algorithm rejects every negative DNF input with certainty and accepts positive inputs with probability at least 2/3. Flipping its answer gives an algorithm with certain acceptance on every positive input of the complement, matching this catalog’s \(\mathrm{R}^{1}(f)\) convention.

Notes

The paper calls its certain-rejection measure $\mathrm{R}_1$; this catalog calls it \(\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{RP}}(f)\). The legacy \(\mathrm{R}^{1}(f)\) entry fixes the other output side. The original and complementary DNF witnesses therefore settle both one-sided columns without identifying the two measures on the same function.
