\(\mathrm{AIKuD}\)

Ambainis–Iraids–Kokainis unambiguous DNF

Log-free optimal separation $\mathrm{C}=\Theta(\mathrm{R}_{\mathrm{RP}}^2)$.

total separating-total arity: $n^2$ bits (even $n$)

Description

Partition $n^2$ bits into $n$ blocks $x_i$. Fix lists $P_j\in[n]^{8n}$ satisfying Lemma 3.1. For a half-sized set $S$, put $m_j(S)=\min\{a:P_j(a)\in S\}$ and $A_{ij}(S)=\{P_i(a):1\le a\le m_j(S)\}$. Term $T_{i,S}$ fixes block $i$ to the characteristic vector of $S$ and requires $x_j(p)=0$ for $j\ne i$ and $p\in A_{ij}(S)$. Accept iff a term is satisfied. The lists guarantee term width at most $9n$; terms are pairwise incompatible.

Formal definition

Fix even $n$, $k=n/2$, and lists $P_j\in[n]^{8n}$ satisfying Lemma 3.1 of [AIK26]. Put $m_j(S)=\min\{a:P_j(a)\in S\}$ and $A_{ij}(S)=\{P_i(a):1\le a\le m_j(S)\}$. $$T_{i,S}(x)=\left(\bigwedge_{p\in S}x_i(p)\right) \left(\bigwedge_{p\notin S}\neg x_i(p)\right) \left(\bigwedge_{j\ne i}\bigwedge_{p\in A_{ij}(S)}\neg x_j(p)\right), \qquad f_n(x)=\bigvee_{i\in[n],\,|S|=k}T_{i,S}(x).$$
