\(f_{\mathrm{AS}}\)

Ambainis-Sun function

Improves Rubinstein's constant to $\mathrm{bs} = \tfrac23 \mathrm{s}^2 - \tfrac13 \mathrm{s}$.

total composed arity: $N=(4k+2)(3k+2)$ bits

Description

An OR of $3k+2$ copies of a gadget $g$ on $n = 4k+2$ bits, exactly as in \(f_{\mathrm{Rub}}\) but with a cleverer gadget: $g$ fires on $2k+1$ cyclic patterns, each of which pins $3k+2$ of the $n$ coordinates (rather than all of them), so the gadget keeps $\mathrm{s}^0(g)=1$ and $\mathrm{bs}^0(g)=n/2$ while raising $\mathrm{s}^1(g)$ from $n$ only to $3k+2 = \tfrac34 n + \tfrac12$. Since the OR level multiplies $\mathrm{s}^0$ and $\mathrm{bs}^0$ by the number of blocks but leaves $\mathrm{s}^1$ alone, taking $3k+2$ blocks balances the two sides and buys the factor $4/3$ over Rubinstein.

Formal definition

$$f(x^1,\dots,x^{3k+2}) = \bigvee_{i=1}^{3k+2} g(x^i), \qquad g(y_1,\dots,y_{4k+2}) = 1 \iff \exists j\in[2k+1]:\ y \sim P_j,$$ where the pattern $P_j$ demands $y_{2j-1}=y_{2j}=1$ and $y_{2j+2i}=y_{2j-2i-1}=y_{2j-2i}=0$ for all $i\in[k]$, indices taken modulo $n=4k+2$. Any two patterns are at Hamming distance $\ge 3$, which is what forces $\mathrm{s}^0(g)=1$.

\(\mathrm{bs}(f)\) Block sensitivity \(6k^2+7k+2\) Attained at the all-zero input: $\mathrm{bs}^0(f) = (3k+2)\cdot\mathrm{bs}^0(g) = (3k+2)(2k+1)$, since flipping the $j$-th pattern's two $1$-bits inside any block turns $f$ on, and blocks from different copies are disjoint. With $\mathrm{s} = 3k+2$ this is exactly $\tfrac23\mathrm{s}^2 - \tfrac13\mathrm{s}$, the current record constant for the quadratic separation. AmbainisSun11
\(\mathrm{s}(f)\) Sensitivity \(3k+2\) Both sides agree: $\mathrm{s}^0(f) = (3k+2)\mathrm{s}^0(g) = 3k+2$ and $\mathrm{s}^1(f) = \mathrm{s}^1(g) = 3k+2$. Balancing them is exactly how the number of blocks was chosen. AmbainisSun11
\(\mathrm{s}^0(f)\) 0-sensitivity \(3k+2\) Sensitivity of an OR of blocks is $n_{\mathrm{blocks}}\cdot\mathrm{s}^0$ of the gadget, and the Ambainis--Sun gadget has $\mathrm{s}^0(g) = 1$: two single-bit flips of a $0$-input landing in different patterns would put those patterns at Hamming distance $\le 2$, contradicting the distance-$3$ property. AmbainisSun11

So the separation is one-sided, as in \(f_{\mathrm{Rub}}\).
