\(F_{k,m}\)

OpenAI’s nearly quartic randomized–quantum family

Settles the optimal total-function $\mathrm{R}$-versus-$\mathrm{Q}$ exponent at $4$.

total composed arity: A winner-column construction on $m$ columns, with address and certificate arrays; $k\ge2$ is fixed and $m$ ranges over sufficiently large powers of four.

Description

Each column contains an $m$-bit string and an address-indexed certificate array. A unique winner has an all-one string and a valid certificate pointing to a zero in every other column. Addresses are computed by instances of the Bansal–Sinha partial-function separation. A quantum tournament finds a short list containing the winner; a randomized algorithm must find an erased zero or the correctly addressed filled cell [OpenAI26QuarticRQ, Sections 2–4].

Notes

The fixed-k separation exponent is $4-2/k$ up to polylogarithmic factors. Varying k proves that the optimal universal power is 4; the paper does not state a fixed-k $\widetilde\Omega(\mathrm{Q}^4)$ lower bound. The general upper bound is $\mathrm{R}\le\mathrm{D}=O(\mathrm{Q}^4)$ [ABKRT21].
