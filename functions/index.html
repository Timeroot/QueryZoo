Partial functions

f : Dom(f) -> {0,1} with Dom(f) a proper subset of the cube (promise problems). Most polynomial relations FAIL here.

\(\mathrm{forr}\) FORRELATION
Forrelation
 quantum One quantum query vs $\widetilde\Theta(\sqrt n)$ randomized queries -- the maximal possible gap at $\mathrm{Q}=1$.
\(\mathrm{forr}_k\) k-FORRELATION
kForrelation
 quantum Optimal $\mathrm{Q}$-vs-$\mathrm{R}$ separation for every $k$: $\mathrm{Q}=\lceil k/2\rceil$, $\mathrm{R}=\widetilde\Theta(n^{1-1/k})$.
\(\mathrm{SIMON}_n\) Simon's problem
Simon
 quantum The first exponential quantum speedup for a promise problem: $\mathrm{Q}=O(n)$ vs $\mathrm{R}=\Omega(2^{n/2})$.
\(\mathrm{DJ}_n\) Deutsch-Jozsa
DeutschJozsa
 quantum Exact quantum: 1 query vs $N/2+1$ deterministic queries (but $\mathrm{R}=O(1)$).
\(\mathrm{BV}_n\) Bernstein-Vazirani
BV
 quantum One exact quantum query recovers an $n$-bit secret that costs $n$ classical queries.
\(\mathrm{SEARCH}^{\le 1}_n\) Unique search
UniqueSearch
 quantum Grover search as a partial function. The reason it is in the zoo: $\deg = 1$ while $\widetilde{\deg} = \Theta(\sqrt n)$, so $\widetilde{\deg} \le \deg$ -- a triviality for total functions -- fails outright on a promise.
\(\mathrm{SEARCH}^{0/2}_n\) Two-or-none search
Search02
 quantum \(\mathrm{SEARCH}^{\le 1}_n\) with the promise moved from $|x|\le 1$ to $|x|\in\{0,2\}$. No two domain points are now adjacent, so $\mathrm{s} = \lambda = 0$, while $\mathrm{Q} = \mathrm{Adv} = \Theta(\sqrt n)$ is untouched.
\(\mathrm{MARK}_n\) Marked bit
MarkedBit
 quantum The certificate barrier at full strength: $\mathrm{C} = \mathrm{C}^0 = \mathrm{C}^1 = 1$, yet $\widetilde{\deg} = \mathrm{Q} = \Theta(\sqrt n)$ and $\mathrm{R} = \mathrm{D} = \Theta(n)$. Recognising the answer is one query; finding it is Grover.
\(\mathrm{DMARK}_n\) Dense marked bit
DenseMarkedBit
 quantum Zero-error randomized cost $O(1)$ against $\deg = \mathrm{Q}_E = \Theta(n)$: the cheapest witness that exact degree is not bounded by any function of $\mathrm{R}_0$ off the promise.
\(\mathrm{COL}_n\) Collision problem
Collision
 quantum $\mathrm{Q}=\Theta(n^{1/3})$ vs $\mathrm{R}=\Theta(\sqrt n)$: a polynomial-only quantum speedup for a promise problem.
\(\mathrm{TSAT}_n\) Ambainis-Belovs function
AmbainisBelovsFn

\(\mathrm{RFS}^g_\ell\) Recursive Fourier sampling
RecursiveFourierSampling

\(\mathrm{GapMaj}^{\delta}_{n}\) Gap-Majority
GapMaj

GapMajConst

\(\mathrm{Find1}^{\mathrm{par}}_{n}\) Parity-labelled FIND-1
Find1Parity

\(\mathrm{ApproxIndex}_k\) Approximate index
ApproxIndex

OrderedSearch
