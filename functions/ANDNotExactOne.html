ANDNotExactOne

AND of complemented EXACT-ONE functions

New quantum-certification lower bounds separate $\mathrm{Q}_0$ from both $\mathrm{Q}$ and rational degree.

total separating-total arity: $m^2$ bits

Description

Partition $m^2$ bits into $m$ blocks of length $m$. Accept iff no block has Hamming weight exactly one.

Formal definition

$f_m=\mathrm{AND}_m\circ(\neg\mathrm{EXACT}_{1,m})$
