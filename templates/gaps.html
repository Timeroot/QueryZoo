Gap report — internal worklist, not linked from anywhere

Every ordered pair of measures, in every setting where both are defined, scored by how much closing it would be worth × how wide open it is. Sorted so the top of the list is where a proof buys the most. Nothing here is a claim about the literature: it is a claim about what this zoo currently records, so a high-scoring row is as likely to mean "nobody entered it" as "nobody knows it" — the undoc flag and the filters below separate the two. Both are worth walking down.
