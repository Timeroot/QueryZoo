Relations to other measures

A row shows what the zoo knows about the two ordered pairs involving \({{ m.symbol }}\). “\({{ m.symbol }}\) in terms of it” is the pair with \({{ m.symbol }}\) on the left (the matrix row); the other column is the reverse. Each cell gives, per setting, the exponent range a,b of the best bounds known — \(\mathrm{M_1} = \Omega(\mathrm{M_2}^{a})\) below and \(\mathrm{M_1} = O(\mathrm{M_2}^{b})\) above, so \(a = b\) means settled — or a glyph where no polynomial bound exists at all. These are derived, not just read off the exponent table, so they are present for pairs the table does not cover. A cell with no entry for a setting is one where the zoo has nothing at that setting, and a — one where the ordered pair is empty in all three; derived marks a direction with no hand-written relation record behind it, which is the common case and not a hole.
