\(\mathrm{NQ}(f)\)

Nondeterministic quantum query complexity

Quantum algorithm with nonzero acceptance probability exactly on 1-inputs.

Definition

The algorithm must accept with some positive probability iff $f(x)=1$. de Wolf showed $\mathrm{NQ}(f) = \lceil \mathrm{ndeg}(f)/2\rceil$ where $\mathrm{ndeg}$ is the nondeterministic degree.

Equivalent / near-equivalent measures

  • \(\mathrm{ndeg}(f)\) $\mathrm{NQ}(f) = \lceil \mathrm{ndeg}(f)/2 \rceil$ deWolf03
