Measures › Polynomial degree
\(\overline{\deg}(f)\)

Exact bounded polynomial degree

Exact degree with the polynomial also constrained to [0,1] on the entire Boolean cube.

also written: bounded-polynomial degree, Delta_0,0,1

Definition

The minimum degree of a multilinear real polynomial $p$ such that $p(x)=f(x)$ on $D$ and $0\le p(x)\le1$ for every $x\in\{0,1\}^n$. This is $\Delta_{0,0,1}$ of [KLLPV26, Definition 7]. It agrees with \(\deg(f)\) on total functions, but the zoo's \(\deg(f)\) imposes no bound off a partial function's promise. This separate id preserves that distinction.

Basic facts

  • $\overline{\deg}\le2\mathrm{Q}_{\mathrm{E}}$ by the acceptance-probability polynomial.
  • Its certification transform is \(\overline{\deg}^{\mathrm{cert}}(f)\).

Equivalent / near-equivalent measures

  • \(\deg(f)\) $\overline{\deg}(f)=\deg(f)$ for total $f$. KLLPV26
