\(\mathrm{MAdv}(f)\)

Multiplicative adversary

A multiplicative-weight version of the adversary method, at least as strong as Adv^pm.

Definition

Replaces the additive progress measure by a multiplicative one; it is at least as strong as $\mathrm{Adv}^{\pm}$ and applies to bounds with small success probability (strong direct product theorems), where $\mathrm{Adv}^{\pm}$ degrades.
