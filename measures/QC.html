\(\mathrm{QC}(f)\)

Quantum certificate complexity

Quantum analogue of certificate complexity; the square root of RC.

Definition

$\mathrm{QC}_x(f)$ is the minimum number of quantum queries needed to verify that the input is $x$ "as far as $f$ is concerned"; $\mathrm{QC}(f)=\max_x \mathrm{QC}_x(f)$.

Basic facts

  • $\mathrm{QC}(f) = \Theta(\sqrt{\mathrm{RC}(f)}) = \Theta(\sqrt{\mathrm{fbs}(f)})$ [Aaronson08].
  • $\mathrm{QC}(f) \le \mathrm{Q}(f)$.
  • Operational certification for $\mathrm{Q}$, quantum distinguishing complexity, the quantum adversary bound, all four quantum sabotage variants, bounded approximate degree and block-multilinear bounded approximate degree equals $\Theta(\mathrm{QC})$ [KLLPV26, Theorems 4 and 6].
  • For every quantum sabotage variant, $\mathrm{QC}=O(\mathrm{QS})$ even on partial functions; Index shows the inequality need not be tight [CMP24, KLLPV26].

Equivalent / near-equivalent measures

  • \(\mathrm{RC}(f)\) $\mathrm{QC}(f) = \Theta(\sqrt{\mathrm{RC}(f)})$ Aaronson08

Graph connectivity total \(\Theta(m) = \Theta(\sqrt N)\) $\mathrm{QC}(f) = \Theta(\sqrt{\mathrm{fbs}(f)})$ [Aaronson08], and $\mathrm{fbs} = \Theta(m^2)$ here. The $1$-side already has a classical certificate of size $m-1$ (a spanning tree); what the quantum verifier compresses is the $\Theta(m^2)$-edge cut on the $0$-side. Aaronson08
