\(\mathrm{fbs}(f)\)

Fractional block sensitivity

LP relaxation of block sensitivity; equals the LP relaxation of certificate complexity by duality.

also written: fractional certificate complexity (LP dual), FC(f)

Definition

$\mathrm{fbs}_x(f)$ is the optimum of the LP $$\max \sum_{B} w_B \quad \text{s.t.}\quad \sum_{B \ni i} w_B \le 1\ \forall i,\quad w_B \ge 0,$$ where $B$ ranges over sensitive blocks of $x$. Its LP dual is the fractional certificate complexity $\mathrm{FC}_x(f)$ (a fractional vertex cover of the blocks), so $\mathrm{fbs} = \mathrm{FC}$ exactly.

Basic facts

  • $\mathrm{bs}(f) \le \mathrm{fbs}(f) \le \mathrm{C}(f)$.
  • $\mathrm{fbs}(f) \le \mathrm{bs}(f)^{?}$ -- the best known separation is $\mathrm{fbs} = \Omega(\mathrm{bs}^{3/2})$ and the best upper bound is $\mathrm{fbs} = O(\mathrm{bs}^2)$ [GSS16].
  • $\mathrm{fbs}(f) = O(\widetilde{\deg}(f)^2)$ [Kulkarni-Tal].

Equivalent / near-equivalent measures

  • \(\mathrm{RC}(f)\) $\mathrm{fbs}(f) = \Theta(\mathrm{RC}(f))$ KulkarniTal16 Aaronson08
  • \(\mathrm{CA}(f)\) $\mathrm{fbs}(f) = \Theta(\mathrm{CA}(f))$ for total $f$ ABIKPS17

Deutsch-Jozsa partial \(2\) putative The fractional relaxation buys nothing: every sensitive block has size exactly $N/2$, so a feasible weighting has $\tfrac N2\sum_B w_B = \sum_i \mathrm{load}(i) \le N$.
