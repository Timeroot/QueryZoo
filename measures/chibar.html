\(\bar{\chi}(f)\)

Max-conflict complexity

A strengthening of conflict complexity that dominates sabotage complexity.

Definition

The "max" version of conflict complexity of Gavinsky-Lee-Santha-Sanyal: the adversary may choose the hard distribution pair after seeing the algorithm's behaviour, yielding a larger and better-behaved measure.

Basic facts

  • $\mathrm{R}(f\circ g^n) = \Omega(\mathrm{R}(f)\,\bar{\chi}(g))$ [GLSS19].
  • $\bar{\chi}(g) = \Omega(\sqrt{\mathrm{R}(g)})$ even for partial $g$ [GLSS19, Thm 6] -- proved there for the smaller measure $\chi$, so it holds a fortiori for $\bar\chi$.
  • $\bar{\chi}(g) = \Omega(\mathrm{RS}(g))$ for all partial $g$ [GLSS19, Thm 7]. The two facts together make the gap unbounded on partial functions: the collision problem has $\mathrm{RS} = \Theta(1)$ but $\mathrm{R} = \Theta(\sqrt n)$, hence $\bar\chi = \Omega(n^{1/4})$. Not being able to collapse that way is the whole point of the measure.
  • Ordering: $\mathrm{RS} \lesssim \bar{\chi} \lesssim \mathrm{LR} \lesssim \mathrm{R}$; no asymptotic separation among the first three is known for total functions.
  • Total families separate $\bar\chi$ from sabotage by $\Omega(\log N)$ and linearised complexity from $\bar\chi$ by $\Omega(\sqrt{\log N})$ [Meiburg26composition]; see \(P_q\) and \(G_q\).

A cell with no entry for a setting is one where the zoo has nothing at that setting, and a — one where the ordered pair is empty in all three; derived marks a direction with no hand-written relation record behind it, which is the common case and not a hole.
