\(\mathrm{CA}(f)\)

Classical adversary bound

The classical analogue of the positive-weights quantum adversary; a lower bound on R.

also written: relational adversary, Aaronson adversary, Laplante-Magniez Kolmogorov bound

Definition

Aaronson's classical adversary: choose a relation $\mathcal{R}$ between 0-inputs and 1-inputs and weights; the bound is the resulting min-max quantity. Several apparently different formulations (Aaronson's, the Laplante-Magniez Kolmogorov version, the "weighted" version) all coincide.

Basic facts

  • $\mathrm{CA}(f) = O(\mathrm{R}(f))$ for every (partial) Boolean $f$.
  • $\mathrm{CA}(f) \le \mathrm{EC}(f)$ always, and $\mathrm{CA}(f) \le \mathrm{RS}(f)$.
  • $\mathrm{EC}(f) \le \mathrm{C}(f)$ for total $f$ only. The proof hands each input the indicator of a minimal certificate as its weight scheme, and checks the constraint on a pair $x,y$ with $f(x) \ne f(y)$ by observing that their certificates must conflict. On a promise problem two certificates can fail to conflict, because the string stitched together from both may simply be outside the domain -- the same step that breaks the \(\mathrm{CG}(f)\) sandwich. On a partial function, prove an $\mathrm{EC}$ upper bound by exhibiting a weighting directly.
  • There are partial functions with $\mathrm{R}(f)$ exponentially larger than $\mathrm{CA}(f)$ [CGLMSG23].

Equivalent / near-equivalent measures

  • \(\mathrm{CG}^{\mathrm{pub}}(f)\) $\mathrm{CA}(f) = \Theta(\mathrm{CG}^{\mathrm{pub}}(f))$ CGLMSG23
  • \(\mathrm{fbs}(f)\) $\mathrm{CA}(f) = \Theta(\mathrm{fbs}(f))$ for total $f$ ABIKPS17

Defined for

  • Total functions yes
    $\mathrm{CA}(f) = \Theta(\mathrm{fbs}(f))$ for total $f$ -- all classical adversary methods are equivalent and equal fractional block sensitivity [ABIKPS17].
  • Partial functions yes
    For partial functions $\mathrm{CA}$ is strictly stronger than $\mathrm{fbs}$ and can be exponentially separated from $\mathrm{R}$ [CGLMSG23].
  • Relations yes
    The relational adversary was designed for exactly this setting.
