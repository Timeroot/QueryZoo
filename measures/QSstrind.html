\(\mathrm{QS}^{\mathrm{ind}}_{\mathrm{str}}(f)\)

Strong quantum sabotage index complexity

Find a sabotaged coordinate with quantum access to both completions and the sabotaged string.

Definition

In the strong oracle model of \(\mathrm{QS}_{\mathrm{str}}(f)\), output an index $i$ with $x_i\ne y_i$ (equivalently $z_i\in\{\ast,\dagger\}$), with probability at least $2/3$.

Basic facts

  • $\mathrm{QS}^{\mathrm{ind}}_{\mathrm{str}}=O(\mathrm{Q}^{3/2})$, even for partial functions [CMP24, Theorem 1.2]. The proof actually uses quantum distinguishing complexity in place of $\mathrm{Q}$.
  • Whether the exponent can be reduced to $1$ is open [CMP24].
  • Its certification transform is $\Theta(\mathrm{QC})$ [KLLPV26, Theorem 4].
