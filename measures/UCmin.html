\(\mathrm{UC}_{\min}(f)\)

Minimum unambiguous certificate complexity

The cheaper of the two one-sided unambiguous certificate complexities.

Definition

$\mathrm{UC}_{\min}(f) = \min\{\mathrm{UC}^0(f),\ \mathrm{UC}^1(f)\}$ [BHT17, Sec. 2.5]: an unambiguous cover is demanded on only one side of the function, whichever side is cheaper. It is not a min over inputs -- $\mathrm{UC}_{\min}$ is still worst-case within the side it picks. It is the quantity in the $\mathrm{C}(f)=\Omega(\mathrm{UC}_{\min}(f)^{1.22})$ separation of [BHT17], which in turn drives the best known $\lambda$-vs-$\mathrm{C}$ separation.

Basic facts

  • $\mathrm{D}(f) \le \mathrm{UC}_{\min}(f)^2$ and $\mathrm{C}(f) \le \mathrm{UC}_{\min}(f)^2$ [BHT17, Sec. 2.7.2]; the communication-complexity analogue is due to Yannakakis and the query-side proof to Goos.
  • $\mathrm{fbs}(f) \le 2\,\mathrm{UC}_{\min}(f) - 1$ [BHT17, Thm 9], and the factor $2$ cannot be removed. This is exactly why the unambiguous-certificate route cannot produce a super-quadratic $\mathrm{bs}$-vs-$\mathrm{s}$ separation.
  • $\mathrm{UC}_{\min}(f) \le \mathrm{UC}(f)$, but $\mathrm{UC}_{\min}$ is not above $\mathrm{C}$ -- only above $\mathrm{fbs}$.

Unambiguous covers restrict: intersecting each subcube of a cover with a subcube $\rho$ leaves an unambiguous cover of $f|_\rho$ of no larger width, so $\mathrm{UC}^b(f|_\rho) \le \mathrm{UC}^b(f)$. Hence $\mathrm{UC}^0, \mathrm{UC}^1 \ge \max\{k,\,n-k+1\}$ and $\mathrm{UC}_{\min} = \Theta(n)$ -- the threshold family cannot witness anything for the [BHT17] $\mathrm{C}$-vs-$\mathrm{UC}_{\min}$ question. Simon's problem partial \(O(2^{n/2})\) Squeezed between $\mathrm{C}_{\min} = 2$ and $\mathrm{UC} = \Theta(2^{n/2})$, and nothing sharper is available. $\mathrm{UC}_{\min} = \min\{\mathrm{UC}^0,\mathrm{UC}^1\}$ is worst-case within the cheaper side, not a minimum over inputs: the cheap two-query collision certificates on the "periodic" side are massively ambiguous, since a periodic $g$ contains $2^{n-1}$ of them, and disambiguating them by "first collision in lex order" costs the full $\Theta(2^{n/2})$ again.
Deutsch-Jozsa partial \(O(N)\) Squeezed between $\mathrm{C}_{\min} = 2$ and $\mathrm{UC} = N/2 + 1$. $\mathrm{UC}_{\min} = \min\{\mathrm{UC}^0,\mathrm{UC}^1\}$ is worst-case within the cheaper side, not a minimum over inputs, so the width-$2$ certificates on the balanced side do not give $O(1)$: a balanced input has $N^2/4$ disagreeing pairs, and an unambiguous family must still name one of them, which is the same obstruction that forces $\mathrm{UC} = \Omega(N)$.

Collision problem partial \(O(n)\) Squeezed between $\mathrm{C}_{\min} = 2$ and $\mathrm{UC} = n/2 + 1$. $\mathrm{UC}_{\min} = \min\{\mathrm{UC}^0,\mathrm{UC}^1\}$ is worst-case within the cheaper side, not a minimum over inputs: a two-to-one $g$ has $n/2$ collision pairs, so the width-$2$ collision certificates overlap heavily and no unambiguous family of constant width covers that side.
Element distinctness total \(O(n)\) Squeezed between $\mathrm{C}_{\min} = 2$ and $\mathrm{UC} = n$. $\mathrm{UC}_{\min} = \min\{\mathrm{UC}^0,\mathrm{UC}^1\}$ is worst-case within the cheaper side, not a minimum over inputs, so the width-$2$ collision certificates (which live on the $0$-side under the convention $\mathrm{ED}_n = 1$ on all-distinct inputs) do not give $O(1)$: an input with many repeats has many such pairs and the family must disambiguate them.

Surjectivity total \(\Omega(n/\log n),\ O(n)\) $\mathrm{UC}^b\ge\mathrm{C}^b$ on each side, so $\mathrm{UC}_{\min}\ge\min\{\mathrm{C}^0,\mathrm{C}^1\}=\Theta(n/\log n)$, and $\mathrm{UC}_{\min}\le\mathrm{UC}=\Theta(n)$. Which side is cheaper is exactly the open part: the $0$-certificates are the short ones. BHT17
Triangle finding total \(\Theta(m^2)\)· putative $\mathrm{UC}^0\ge\mathrm{C}^0=\binom m2$, so the question is the $1$-side. For the input consisting of a single triangle $T$, any certificate must fix a triangle to $1$ and can only fix edges of $T$, so its $1$-part is exactly $T$. For two distinct triangles $T$ and $U$ the graph $T\cup U$ then lies in both certificates unless one of them fixes an edge of the other to $0$; orienting each such pair, some triangle beats at least half the others, and since each edge lies in $m-2$ triangles its $0$-set has size $\Omega(m^2)$. Hence $\mathrm{UC}^1=\Theta(m^2)$ too, and the ceiling is $\mathrm{UC}=\binom m2$. BHT17
Ordered search partial \(2\) $\mathrm{UC}_{\min}=\min\{\mathrm{UC}^0,\mathrm{UC}^1\}$ [BHT17], and both sides are $2$ by the family of the \(\mathrm{UC}(f)\) entry; the floor is $\mathrm{C}_{\min}$ on the relevant side. BHT17
Inner product mod 2 total \(n/2\) putative That same partition gives the input $x = 0^m$ a certificate of size $m$, and no certificate anywhere is smaller than $\mathrm{C}_{\min} = m$.
Minsky-Papert one-in-a-box total \(N = m^3\) putative The parity argument recorded for \(\mathrm{UC}(f)\) works on both sides, so the one-sided relaxation buys nothing. $|f^{-1}(1)| = (2^{m^2}-1)^m$ is odd, and $|f^{-1}(0)| = 2^{N} - (2^{m^2}-1)^m$ is even minus odd, hence also odd. An unambiguous $b$-certificate family partitions $f^{-1}(b)$ into subcubes of sizes $2^{N-w_i}$; an odd total forces some $w_i = N$. So $\mathrm{UC}^0 = \mathrm{UC}^1 = N$ and $\mathrm{UC}_{\min} = N = m^3$, against $\mathrm{C} = m^2$. This is an extreme point of $\mathrm{C} \ge \mathrm{UC}_{\min}^{1.22}$-type separations in the opposite direction: here $\mathrm{UC}_{\min}$ is as far above $\mathrm{C}$ as $\mathrm{D} \le \mathrm{UC}_{\min}^2$ allows it to be useful at all.
Sink of a tournament total \(\Theta(m) = \Theta(\sqrt N)\) Sandwiched: $\mathrm{C}_{\min} \le \mathrm{UC}_{\min} \le \mathrm{UC}$, with $\mathrm{C}_{\min} = m-1$ and $\mathrm{UC} = \Theta(m)$ here.
Scorpion graphs total \(\Theta(m)\) Sandwiched: $\mathrm{C}_{\min} \le \mathrm{UC}_{\min} \le \mathrm{UC}$, with $\mathrm{C}_{\min} = m-1$ and $\mathrm{UC} = \Theta(m)$ here.
Graph connectivity total \(\Theta(m^2) = \Theta(N)\) putative The one-sided relaxation buys nothing, but for a softer reason than on \(\mathrm{MP}_m\): the $0$-side is genuinely cheap to cover unambiguously and it is still $\Theta(N)$. Cover: for each connected vertex set $S \ni v_1$ with $S \ne V$, take the certificate that specifies every edge inside $S$ together with all $|S|(m-|S|)$ cross edges absent. Each disconnected graph matches exactly one of these -- $S$ is forced to be the component of $v_1$ -- so $\mathrm{UC}^0 \le N$, and $\mathrm{UC}^0 \ge \mathrm{C}^0 = \Theta(m^2)$. The lower bound on the min is $\mathrm{fbs} \le 2\,\mathrm{UC}_{\min} - 1$ (\(\mathrm{fbs}(f)\) vs \(\mathrm{UC}_{\min}(f)\)) with $\mathrm{fbs} = \Theta(m^2)$.
